# AI Flow Chat

The [flow editor](./1_flow_editor.mdx) includes AI Flow Chat, an integrated AI chat panel that lets you create and modify flows using natural language. Describe what you want to achieve and the AI builds the flow step by step: it adds scripts, [loops](./12_flow_loops.md) and [branches](./13_flow_branches.md), searches your workspace and [Windmill Hub](https://hub.windmill.dev/) for relevant scripts or generates new ones, and connects steps by filling in inputs based on context.
