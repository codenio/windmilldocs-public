# Contact / Getting help

Getting stuck using Windmill? Your problem can be quickly solved and reporting it is the best way to contribute!

First of all, you could join our [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/V7PM2YHsPB), from where the team and even other contributors will be happy to give you a hand.

From GitHub, feel free to open a [bug report](https://github.com/windmill-labs/windmill/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml), or a [feature request](https://github.com/windmill-labs/windmill/issues/new?template=feature_request.md) for a broader idea. We are not seeking outside code contribution at the moment, see the [Contributor guide](../4_contributing/index.md) for what we do accept.

Also, you might want to reach out by email at contact@windmill.dev.

For more in-depth discussions, reach out at sales@windmill.dev or schedule a [meeting](https://www.windmill.dev/book-demo) with the founder.
